SMP-Video from the SMP Challenge (smpchallenge/SMP-Video on Hugging Face, gated): short TikTok-style videos posted between Jan 2022 and Aug 2023. This page describes the whole labelled train file, 4,000 posts. The dataset’s test files hold 2,000 more posts with no popularity label, so they are not used. The label popularity is a continuous score (mean 8.26, SD 2.86). Its 90th percentile is 11.69 (400 posts at or above it). No train/test split is defined yet.

Every post joins to its user and video metadata, with no gaps. 324 users have more than one post (up to 12), so the 4,000 posts come from 3,488 users, and user columns repeat for those users. Likes per video (user likes divided by video count) tracks popularity best of the simple features, with Spearman 0.65.
