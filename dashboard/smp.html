SMP-Video from the SMP Challenge (smpchallenge/SMP-Video on Hugging Face, gated): short TikTok-style videos posted between Jan 2022 and Aug 2023. Only the train split has labels, so the 1,500 rows used here are the first 1,500 of its 4,000 labelled posts, split 1,000 / 500 with a seeded shuffle. The target is popularity, a continuous score (median 8.75), so this is regression, not YES/NO like the other benchmarks.

Columns

22 rows below cover all 24 columns. Bold columns hold text (free text or a short category string); the rest are numbers, a timestamp, a file path or IDs. Of the model-usable columns, 3 are free text, 4 are categories and 11 are numbers.
