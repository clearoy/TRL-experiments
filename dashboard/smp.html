SMP-Video from the SMP Challenge (smpchallenge/SMP-Video on Hugging Face, gated): short TikTok-style videos posted between Jan 2022 and Aug 2023. This page describes the whole labelled train file, 4,000 posts. The dataset’s test files hold 2,000 more posts with no popularity label, so they are not used. The label popularity is a continuous score (mean 8.26, SD 2.86). Its 90th percentile is 11.69 (400 posts at or above it). No train/test split is defined yet.

Every post joins to its user and video metadata, with no gaps. 324 users have more than one post (up to 12), so the 4,000 posts come from 3,488 users, and user columns repeat for those users. Likes per video (user likes divided by video count) tracks popularity best of the simple features, with Spearman 0.65.

2  Clean dataset

2,192posts (US and English)
1,942distinct users
5user groups, about 438 posts each
17columns, user counts removed

Built by smp/extract_clean.py from the 4,000 labelled posts, with each post’s video copied alongside. The train/test split is in section 3.

Steps

  1. Filter: keep posts with post_location = US and post_text_language = en (2,192 of 4,000). Nothing else is removed.
  2. Group by account: predict popularity from the account alone, then cut that prediction into five equal groups, so posts are compared with posts from similar accounts. 
    expected_popularity = 3.84 + 1.64 × log10(followers)
                    − 1.26 × log10(videos posted)
                    − 0.10 × log10(following)
    Likes, heart and digg counts are left out: they partly include the post’s own likes.
  3. Drop the account columns: the 7 user_* counts are removed, so only the group carries account information. post_location and post_text_language are removed too, being the same on every row.

Why this grouping

Seven ways to group the 2,192 posts by account. “Variance removed” is how much popularity differs between groups; “follower ρ inside groups” is the account effect left over within a group (lower is better).

3  Split

1,749 / 443train / test posts (about 80 / 20)
0users on both sides
Top 20%label, within each group
5folders, one per group

Built by smp/prepare_data.py into smp/data/split/group_1 … group_5, each with train.parquet and test.parquet.

Steps

  1. Split by user: all of a user’s posts go to one side. The split is stratified by follower quintile, so every account tier appears in both train and test.
  2. Refit the groups on train only: the group model is fitted on the 1,749 training posts (intercept 3.93, followers +1.62, videos -1.23, following -0.12), and its group edges come from training posts. Test posts get the same model and edges.
  3. Label inside each group: YES = the top 20% of popularity among the group’s training posts. The same cutoff is applied to the group’s test posts.
